గుర్తుచేయడం Reminders

Every reminder, every year, automatically: download the file, then in Google Calendar on a computer go to Settings → Import and pick it. Each festival and family tithi appears on its day with the things to do, and Google reminds you on your phone. Set all-day notifications to "1 day before" in Google Calendar settings. Or tap Remind me on any single day.

WhatsApp: the WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the message typed, and you choose who gets it. For an automatic message every morning, the daily WhatsApp job sends today's tithi, festivals and your notes. After you add or change family dates or notes, tap Download data for daily WhatsApp and upload the file to the job's data folder on GitHub.

How days are worked out: sun and moon positions are calculated on this page, using the amanta Telugu calendar and Lahiri ayanamsa. A day's tithi is the one running at sunrise. Festivals follow their traditional time of day: midday for Sri Rama Navami and Vinayaka Chavithi, afternoon for Dasara, evening for Deepavali, midnight for Shivaratri and Krishnashtami. In a year with an adhika month, the regular month is used. Families and temples sometimes choose the neighbouring day, so check close calls with your panchangam.
